How to Choose a Game

Before installing a game, check whether it supports your device, operating system and available storage. Review its age rating, system requirements, in-app purchases and internet requirements.

For safety, use official game publishers and trusted app stores. Some games are free to play, while others may require payment or offer optional in-game purchases.

Safety tip: MediaHub provides discovery links. Always review the destination website before downloading or installing any game.
