How to Choose Safe Mobile Apps

Before installing an app, check its developer, reviews, permissions, device compatibility and privacy policy. Download applications from official websites or trusted app stores.

Some applications are free, while others offer subscriptions, paid features or in-app purchases. Review the terms before paying.

Safety tip: MediaHub helps users discover apps. Always check the official source before installing an application.
