How to Choose an AI Tool

Before using an AI service, review its features, supported languages, privacy policy, usage limits and pricing. Some services offer free access with optional paid plans.

Do not enter passwords, financial details or other sensitive personal information into an AI tool unless you understand how the service handles that information.
