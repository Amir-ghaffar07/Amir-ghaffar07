How to Download Software Safely

Before installing software, check the official developer, supported operating systems, system requirements and license. Review whether the program is free, paid or available through a subscription.

Avoid suspicious download buttons and installers from unknown websites. Keep your operating system and security software updated.

Safety tip: MediaHub helps users discover software resources. Always verify the publisher and download applications from their official websites.
